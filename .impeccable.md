## Design Context

### Users
Developers and "vibe coders" on Mac who talk to AI coding assistants. They land from the Mac App Store listing or a search; they want proof it is private, fast, and what it does in 10 seconds.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, direct, trustworthy. Three words: quick, honest, slanted (Montserrat italic energy). Emerald/forest palette from the app (Theme.swift).

### Aesthetic Direction
Forest-green editorial with a single emerald accent. Light and dark via prefers-color-scheme. No cards, no purple, no Inter, no gradient text. Left-aligned asymmetric layout; an HTML mock of the result window as the signature visual.

### Design Principles
1. Show the transformation (messy speech -> clean prompt) before explaining it.
2. Privacy is a first-class section, not a footnote.
3. Accessible by default: AA contrast, visible focus, reduced motion.
4. Zero external requests: self-hosted fonts, no JS dependencies.
